# Returns and exchanges

- **Return window:** 30 days from delivery. Trail Club **Summit** members get 60 days.
- **Condition:** items must be unused, with original tags. Defective items are handled under the warranty policy instead.
- **Opened electronics** (headlamps, GPS units, power banks): a 15% restocking fee applies, unless the item is defective.
- **Final Sale items** cannot be returned or exchanged.
- **Return shipping:** free for Summit members and for defective items. For everyone else, $6.95 is deducted from the refund.
- **Refunds** go to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the item.
- **Exchanges:** one free exchange per order, for a different size or color of the same product, within the return window.
- **Gift returns:** refunded as store credit to the gift recipient.
- **How to start a return:** through your account's Orders page, or by asking support with your order number and email.
