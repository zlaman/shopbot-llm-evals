# Trail Club loyalty program

- **Tiers:**
  - **Trail:** free; every account joins automatically.
  - **Summit:** reached by spending $500 or more within 12 months. Status lasts for the following 12 months.
- **Points:** Trail members earn 1 point per $1 spent. Summit members earn 2 points per $1. Points are earned on the amount paid after discounts, excluding tax and shipping.
- **Rewards:** 100 points = a $5 reward. Rewards can be combined with one promo code per order.
- **Expiry:** points expire 12 months after they are earned.
- **Summit benefits:** 60-day returns, free return shipping, and early access to seasonal sales.
- Returned items deduct the points earned on them.

# Privacy and support

- **Order details:** support shares order details only after verifying the email address and order number on the order.
- **We never ask** for your full card number or password in chat or email.
- **Data deletion:** email privacy@pebbleandpine.example. Requests are processed within 30 days.
- We do not sell personal data.
- **Human support hours:** Monday–Friday, 8 am–6 pm Eastern Time. Email: help@pebbleandpine.example.
- Pebble & Pine is online-only. There are no physical stores.

# Returns and exchanges

- **Return window:** 30 days from delivery. Trail Club **Summit** members get 60 days.
- **Condition:** items must be unused, with original tags. Defective items are handled under the warranty policy instead.
- **Opened electronics** (headlamps, GPS units, power banks): a 15% restocking fee applies, unless the item is defective.
- **Final Sale items** cannot be returned or exchanged.
- **Return shipping:** free for Summit members and for defective items. For everyone else, $6.95 is deducted from the refund.
- **Refunds** go to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the item.
- **Exchanges:** one free exchange per order, for a different size or color of the same product, within the return window.
- **Gift returns:** refunded as store credit to the gift recipient.
- **How to start a return:** through your account's Orders page, or by asking support with your order number and email.

# Shipping

- **Free standard shipping** on orders of $75 or more, counted after discounts and before tax. Below $75, standard shipping costs $5.95.
- **Standard shipping:** 3–5 business days. **Expedited:** 1–2 business days for $14.95.
- **Where we ship:** the 48 contiguous US states and Canada. Alaska and Hawaii: expedited only, $24.95. We do not ship to other countries.
- **Same-day dispatch:** orders placed before 1 pm Eastern Time on a business day ship that day.
- **Tracking:** emailed when the order ships.
- **Lost or damaged packages:** report within 14 days of the expected delivery date and we will send a replacement or refund.
- **Address changes:** possible only before the order ships.

# Warranty

- **Pebble & Pine branded tents and backpacks:** 3-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
- **Other Pebble & Pine branded gear:** 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
- **Third-party brands:** covered by the manufacturer's warranty. We help you contact them.
- **Not covered:** normal wear and tear, misuse, accidents, alterations, or damage from improper care.
- **How to claim:** send your order number and photos of the defect to support. We repair, replace or issue store credit, at our discretion.
- **Proof of purchase:** required. Orders from our website count automatically.
- Warranty claims do not extend the original return window.
